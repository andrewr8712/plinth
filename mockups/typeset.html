Type system

One family, three weights, seven sizes. Inter variable is bundled in the APK rather than loaded at runtime, because every clamp and line count in this design depends on its metrics — and the system stack cannot promise them: on Android the 64 px hairline was never a 200 at all.

The old rule dropped 64 → 48 by character count, which fired on titles that already fit (measured: “The Court of the Crimson King” is 832 px at 64 against 992 px of two-line capacity). The step is now a real 1.45× and it is triggered by measured width, not a character count. Long titles still land exactly two lines, so the transport below them moves by one line — 48 px — instead of the 63 px drift measured across states.

What ships in the APK

One file, Inter variable, 47 KB WOFF2 / ≈250 KB TTF subset to Latin + Latin Extended, in res/font. Three pinned instances at 300 / 400 / 500 — no 600, no 700; emphasis on this surface is size and colour, not weight. Tabular figures are declared on the data and numeric roles, because a sample-rate readout that jitters as digits change forfeits the whole idea of an instrument. Sizes are sp, so they follow the user's text scale; the 2 m legibility floor is 16 sp for anything that must be read from the couch, and 11 sp labels are texture at that distance by design.
